marmalade TTS

Give your phone a voice worth listening to.

Fast, natural, private text-to-speech for your whole phone. Works offline. Set Marmalade as your system text-to-speech engine and every app that reads aloud — screen readers, e-readers, chat apps — speaks through it.

Get it on F-Droid GitHub releases (APK)

Free, open source, no ads, no accounts.

Features

  • Natural neural voices. Ready to speak the moment you install.
  • Fast. Speech responds quickly even on modest phones.
  • Private by default. Everything runs on your device.
  • Works everywhere. A drop-in replacement for Google or Samsung TTS — pick it once in Settings.
  • A voice for every app. Route each app to its own voice, speed, and effects.
  • Speaks 8 languages. Auto-detects the language being read on the Kokoro engine.
  • Reads the web. Share a link and Marmalade reads the article aloud, highlighting as it goes.
  • Voice effects. 22 presets like 8-bit, cyborg, megaphone, and vintage radio — plus an editor to make your own.
  • Accessible. Rich TalkBack support, designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Optional cloud voices. From private third parties with your own API key (Venice.ai).

Languages

The multilingual Kokoro engine speaks English (US and UK), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Japanese, and Mandarin Chinese. It detects the language as it reads and switches pronunciation on the fly.

  • English (US)

  • Hindi
  • Japanese

More languages are in the works, with an emphasis on ethically trained voices — clear licenses, consenting speakers, disclosed training data.

See the language support status page for where each requested language stands, and if you speak German, please review our German voice samples — your feedback decides whether the German voice ships.

Private, with or without the cloud

No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no backend of ours. On-device voices touch the network only to download the engines you pick. If you want voices beyond what your phone can run, add a cloud engine with your own API key. Until you set one up, nothing you read ever leaves your phone.

License

Marmalade's own source code is MIT licensed and its repository contains no GPL code. The published app also contains the espeak-ng phonemizer, compiled from source at build time, so the app you install is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The exact espeak-ng version used is pinned from the project repository, and license details for every component are shown inside the app.
