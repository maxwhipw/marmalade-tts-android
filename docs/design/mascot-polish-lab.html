The progression

Each card keeps everything from the one before and fixes one layer. Nothing about the character changes — squat amber jar, white ridged lid, big sparkle eyes, gentle smile, cream label with the orange slice. The finish line (P4) is the official candidate: same jar, tightened until it holds up at 24 px and at 512.

P0 Baseline (as-is)

  • The current concept1 SVG, verbatim (serif label text and all).

P1 Geometry

  • Lid, neck, and body centered on one axis; radii from one scale (8/16/24/44).
  • Lid slightly wider than neck, body slightly wider than lid — a proper silhouette taper.
  • Shadow shrunk + softened; jar no longer "floats" on a saucer.
  • Glass shine becomes a capsule inset from the corner, not a corner-clipping rect.

P2 Color & material

  • Jar gradient warmed toward the brand ramp (#F7AC2B → #D9820F); marmalade fill gets a gentle wavy meniscus instead of a hard rect.
  • Rim-light capsule on the right edge — glass reads round, not flat.
  • Label and lid share one cream (#FFF8EE); neck warmed to sit between lid and body.

P3 Face

  • Eyes nudged onto a facial grid (level with each other, 96 units apart) and 5% bigger.
  • Smile thickened to 5 — at app sizes the 2.5 stroke disappears.
  • Blush restored to visible (#FB7185 @ 30% — in the app drawable it exported at 8% and vanished).
  • Highlight pattern standardized: big top-right + small bottom-left, both eyes.

P4 Detail discipline → official candidate

  • Hairline details replaced by fewer, chunkier ones: 6 lid ridges → rounded strokes (w=3) that survive 24 px.
  • Label: serif Georgia → brand wordmark face (Fredoka 600, lowercase, deep amber) with clear space; decorative rules dropped.
  • Orange slice enlarged (r=14), segments thickened, sits half-overlapping the label like a wax seal.
  • Face lifted 6 units — more chin, reads younger/friendlier.

Does it survive small? (P4 at app + launcher sizes)

At 24 px the P0 details (hairline lid lines, label rules, 2.5-weight smile) turn to noise; P4's chunkier strokes and the single wordmark hold. The dark row matters because the app's dark surface (#1c1917) is where the mascot lives half the time.
