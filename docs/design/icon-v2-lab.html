What changed

The waves were off-centre. In the shipped V2 the arcs sit at canvas y 60 while the jar's visual centre lands near y 54 — about 6 units low — and the jar+waves group as a whole sat right of the canvas centre. Every variant here recomputes the layout: the arcs are anchored to a chosen line on the jar, and the whole composition is centred on x 54.

All gradients interpolate between locked palette shades — no new hexes. Orange #F97316, deep amber #C2410C, marmalade amber #D97706, peach #FDBA74, lid cream #FFF8EE, toast #FED7AA.

Notes

Portability: minSdk is 28, so vector gradients port straight into ic_launcher_foreground.xml (and a gradient background needs the adaptive icon's <background> to become a drawable instead of a colour). SVG drop-shadow filters do not port — VectorDrawable has no filter support, so the shadow here is a real offset duplicate path in deep amber at low alpha, which does port 1:1.
