Brief

The mark is settled (V5-e5 in the icon lab). This page tunes the composition only: jar bigger and into the bottom-left corner, bubble staying top-right. Face, colors and field are fixed.

The round mask is the binding constraint. Launchers show roughly the center 72 of the 108 canvas as a circle, so the top-right corner — exactly where the bubble lives — is the first thing cut. Growing the jar without pulling the bubble inward costs you the tail and the top waveform bars (see the P-round). The Q-round compensates.

P — grow & push (naive)

Jar scaled up and moved toward the corner; bubble pushed further out into its corner. Watch the round column — the bubble starts losing its tail.

Q — grow & compensate

Same jar growth, but the bubble is scaled down and pulled inward as the jar grows, so it survives the circle intact.

Shelf test — 48 px

The decision distance. A composition that mushes here is out, however good it looks at 130 px.
