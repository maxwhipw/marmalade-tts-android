Positioning — the rule this page follows

Play's Metadata policy bars unverifiable superlatives ("best TTS app", "#1"). The play here is enumeration as the argument: concrete counts and features nobody else can list — 60+ on-device voices · 3 engine families · 9 languages · 22 editable effect presets · emoji-driven emotion · a different voice per app · open source. The reader does the "so it's the most capable one" math themselves. Every number on this page comes from the shipped catalogs (EngineCatalog.kt: Kokoro 53 voices/9 languages, Kitten 8, Pocket 6; BuiltinEffects.kt: 22 presets).

C · Screenshot treatments

# screenshot frames (no alpha, PNG24, 1200x2400 = exactly 2.0 ratio):
inkscape -w 1200 -h 2400 shot-c1.svg -o /tmp/s.png
magick /tmp/s.png -alpha off PNG24:play-assets/screenshots/01-speak.png
Manrope + Fredoka must be installed locally for inkscape to render the SVG text (fc-list | grep -iE 'manrope|fredoka' to check; both are on Google Fonts, OFL-licensed). The lab previews load them from Google Fonts, so the browser view is the ground truth.
