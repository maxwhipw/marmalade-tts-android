SPEAKS 8 LANGUAGES
With the Kokoro engine, Marmalade speaks English (US and UK), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Japanese, and Mandarin Chinese. It detects the language of each utterance automatically and switches pronunciation on the fly — a Kokoro voice reads Spanish text with Spanish pronunciation without you touching a setting. Kitten and Pocket voices are English-only.
