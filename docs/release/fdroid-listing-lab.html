Intro paragraph — REVISED (last sentence)

Was: "…works entirely on-device… No cloud, no account, no tracking."

Marmalade TTS turns your phone into a private, high-quality text-to-speech engine. Register it as your system TTS engine and every app that reads text aloud — your screen reader, e-reader, podcast or article reader, accessibility tools, and AI chat apps — can speak through Marmalade. Voices run on-device by default. No account, no tracking, no ads.

VOICE ALIASES — REVISED + MOVED UP (personas out, routing in)

Your calls: they're just "aliases", no "personas"; desktop reference dropped; per-app routing added — and both reviewers agreed it's the distinguishing feature, so it moved from 9th place to right after ACCESSIBLE BY DESIGN. ⚠ Reviewer flag: routing is device-verified visually but cross-app routing has never been functionally tested end-to-end — test two routed apps before sign-off, or we soften the sentence.

VOICE ALIASES
Save named voice setups — a voice plus its speed and effects — and switch between them instantly. Route apps to aliases: your e-reader can speak in one voice while your chat app uses another, automatically, per app. Everything unrouted follows your primary alias.

ON-DEVICE NEURAL VOICES — REVISED

Was: "The app ships small and bundles no model files" — false since 2026-08-04: Kitten Nano is baked into the APK (assets/engines-seed/kitten-direct-v0_8, 58 MB) so the app speaks offline out of the box. Turned the inaccuracy into the selling point.

ON-DEVICE NEURAL VOICES
Speech is synthesized locally using neural voice models, and a fast English voice is built in — Marmalade speaks out of the box, fully offline. Bigger engines and voices download only when you choose to install them, and text read by on-device voices never leaves your phone.

New section: SPEAKS 8 LANGUAGES

Your 2026-08-04 note + the auto-detect feature. Inserted after MULTIPLE ENGINES AND VOICES. Verified: the 8-language list matches KokoroDirectVoiceCatalog exactly. SPEAKS 8 LANGUAGES
With the multilingual Kokoro engine, Marmalade speaks English (US and UK), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Japanese, and Mandarin Chinese. It detects the language automatically as it reads and switches pronunciation on the fly — Spanish text gets Spanish pronunciation without you touching a setting.

New section: OPTIONAL CLOUD VOICES — reworded after review

Both reviewers hit the same problem: the bundled provider list (assets/cloud-providers.json) ships OpenAI alongside Venice, so "built around privacy-respecting providers" would read as spin to a traffic-literate F-Droid user one screen deep. Reworded: the privacy note now attaches to Venice alone, phrased as their stated policy ("says it does not…"), not our guarantee. ⚠ Also flagged: opening the Cloud tab fires the provider-list refresh (a GET to GitHub) before any opt-in — decide whether to gate that behind the disclaimer pre-tag.

OPTIONAL CLOUD VOICES
Want voices beyond what your phone can run? Add a cloud engine with your own API key. Providers include Venice.ai, which says it does not store or log requests. Cloud engines are strictly opt-in: until you add one yourself, nothing you have read ever leaves your phone.

EXPRESSIVE, EMOTION-AWARE SPEECH — REVISED + your call

You asked "we do not have emoji text?" — we do, it's live: SynthesisPipeline runs EmojiProsody.detect() on every utterance and ProsodyApplier shapes the audio (pitch/rate/volume per emotion; 11 emoji: 😍😡😎😭🙄😁🙂🤣😮😅🤔) on all engines, in the app and as system TTS. It's a simple audio-shaping pass, not model-level acting — so the copy is toned down to what it honestly does. Ear-check it before sign-off (same sentence with and without 😭); if you don't hear it or don't like it, we cut this section.

EXPRESSIVE, EMOTION-AWARE SPEECH
Emoji shape the delivery. Add an emoji like 😁 or 😭 and Marmalade shifts the pitch, pace, and energy of the spoken audio to match the mood — on any voice.

AUDIO EFFECTS — REVISED

Was: "presets (such as cave, robot, and telephone)" — there is no "robot" preset. The app ships 22 (BuiltinEffects.kt) plus a full chain editor (screenshot 06).

AUDIO EFFECTS
Give spoken output character with over 20 built-in effect presets — cave, telephone, megaphone, vintage radio, dragon, and more — or build your own effect chains in the editor.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN — REVISED

Was: "…no backend. The only network use is downloading the engine files you choose to install." False for cloud engines.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no accounts, and no backend of our own. On-device voices use the network only to download the engine files you choose. Cloud voices talk only to the provider you configured, using your own key — nothing passes through us. See the in-app privacy policy for details.
