Marmalade TTS

Summary — REVISED

Was: "Offline neural text-to-speech engine for your whole device. No cloud." — "No cloud" is no longer true.

Private neural text-to-speech for your whole device. On-device by default.

Intro paragraph — REVISED (last sentence)

Was: "…works entirely on-device… No cloud, no account, no tracking." Reframed: private first, on-device by default.

Marmalade TTS turns your phone into a private, high-quality text-to-speech engine. Register it as your system TTS engine and every app that reads text aloud — your screen reader, e-reader, podcast or article reader, accessibility tools, and AI chat apps — can speak through Marmalade. Voices run on-device by default. No account, no tracking, no ads.

New section: SPEAKS 8 LANGUAGES

Your 2026-08-04 note: name the languages; plus the auto-detect feature that shipped since. Inserted after MULTIPLE ENGINES AND VOICES.

SPEAKS 8 LANGUAGES
With the Kokoro engine, Marmalade speaks English (US and UK), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Japanese, and Mandarin Chinese. It detects the language of each utterance automatically and switches pronunciation on the fly — a Kokoro voice reads Spanish text with Spanish pronunciation without you touching a setting.

New section: OPTIONAL CLOUD VOICES

The Cloud tab exists; the listing has to own it. Framed as opt-in + privacy-respecting. Check the Venice claim wording before sign-off — "doesn't store your text" is their promise, so the copy attributes it rather than asserting it ourselves.

OPTIONAL CLOUD VOICES
Want voices beyond what your phone can run? Add a cloud engine with your own API key. Marmalade is built around privacy-respecting providers — such as Venice.ai, which is designed not to store or log your requests. Cloud engines are strictly opt-in: until you add one yourself, the text you have read never leaves your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN — REVISED

Was: "…no backend. The only network use is downloading the engine files you choose to install." Now false for cloud engines.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no accounts, and no backend of our own. On-device voices use the network only to download the engine files you choose. Cloud voices talk only to the provider you configured, using your own key — nothing passes through us. See the in-app privacy policy for details.
