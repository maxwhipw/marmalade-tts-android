Asset audit

App iconfixed
32-bit PNG (with alpha) · 512px by 512px · Maximum file size: 1024KB

The committed icon.png was 512×512 but 24-bit, no alpha channel. Regenerated as PNG32. 15 KB.

Also per spec: full square, no rounded corners and no drop shadow — Google Play dynamically applies a rounded corner mask with a radius equivalent to 30% of icon size The preview above applies that 30% mask, so what you see is what users see.

Phone screenshots ×5fixed
JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha) · Minimum dimension: 320px · Maximum dimension: 3840px · maximum dimension of your screenshot can't be more than twice as long as minimum dimension

Two violations. They carried an alpha channel (spec says none), and at 1080×2400 the ratio is 2.222 — over the hard 2.0 limit, because a 20:9 phone screen is taller than Play allows. Play rejects these.

Fixed by padding the width 1080 → 1200 on the app's own background, giving exactly 2.000. Padding rather than cropping, so no UI is lost.

The committed short and full descriptions say the app has no cloud and that text never leaves your phone. Both stopped being true on 2026-07-24. This is the most public surface carrying the claim, and Play's Metadata policy covers misleading descriptions.

Regenerate

cd fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images
# icon: add the alpha channel
convert icon.png -alpha set PNG32:out/icon-512.png
# screenshots: drop alpha, pad to 2.000 ratio
convert shot.png -background "#12100e" \
  -alpha remove -alpha off \
  -gravity center -extent 1200x2400 \
  PNG24:out/shot.png
