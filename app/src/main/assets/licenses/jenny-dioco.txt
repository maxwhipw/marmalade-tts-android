Jenny (Dioco) — English VITS Marmalade voice pack (en-jenny_dioco-medium)

This pack is an independent, from-scratch VITS model. Two custom
attribution licences apply: one to the model weights and one to the
training dataset. Both permit commercial use. The dataset's attribution
term dictates that the voice be named "Jenny (Dioco)".


MODEL WEIGHTS

Trained from scratch by Bryce Beattie (https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/).
Licence, verbatim from the trainer's page:

    Attribution required, similar to CC BY
    See the link for license details. I impose no further license or
    restrictions.

The page footer adds:

    Feel free to use these for any legal and ethical purpose. If somebody
    wants to upload these to HuggingFace or somewhere similar, you have my
    blessing.


TRAINING DATASET

jenny-tts-dataset, recorded and released by Jenny (dioco-group)
(https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset). Licence, verbatim from
the dataset README:

    Attribution is required in software/websites/projects/interfaces
    (including voice interfaces) that generate audio in response to user
    action using this dataset. Atribution means: the voice must be referred
    to as "Jenny", and where at all practical, "Jenny (Dioco)". Attribution
    is not required when distributing the generated clips (although
