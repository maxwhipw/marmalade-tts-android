Marmalade TTS turns your phone into a private, high-quality text-to-speech engine that works entirely on-device. Register it as your system TTS engine and every app that reads text aloud — your screen reader, e-reader, podcast or article reader, accessibility tools, and AI chat apps — can speak through Marmalade. No cloud, no account, no tracking.

WORKS EVERYWHERE ON YOUR DEVICE
Marmalade implements Android's standard text-to-speech engine interface, so it's a drop-in replacement for Google or Samsung TTS. Pick it once in Settings and it powers spoken text across your apps.

ON-DEVICE NEURAL VOICES
Speech is synthesized locally using neural voice models. The text you have it read never leaves your phone. The app ships small and bundles no model files: on first launch you choose which engines to install, and each downloads only when you opt in.

MULTIPLE ENGINES AND VOICES
Choose the trade-off that fits you — a tiny, fast engine that runs on any device, or larger high-quality and multilingual voices. Each engine offers several distinct voices, and the catalog grows over time.

EXPRESSIVE, EMOTION-AWARE SPEECH
Emoji in your text drive emotional prosody, so the same words can sound cheerful, worried, or calm depending on how you write them — even when the base voice is neutral.

AUDIO EFFECTS
Apply built-in audio-effect presets (such as cave, robot, and telephone) to give spoken output character.

VOICE ALIASES / PERSONAS
Save named voice "personas" — a voice plus its preprocessing and effect settings — and switch between them instantly, just like the Marmalade desktop tool.

VOICE CLONING (SUPPORTED ENGINES)
Create a custom voice from a short recording. Cloned voices are stored on-device only and never uploaded. The app asks you to confirm you have permission before cloning any voice.

LONG-FORM READING
A foreground playback service reads long text reliably, with lock-screen and Bluetooth transport controls and the screen off.

HANDY SHORTCUTS
Share text to Marmalade from any app, use the system "read selection" action, or speak your clipboard from a Quick Settings tile.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no accounts, and no backend. The only network use is downloading the engine files you choose to install. See the in-app privacy policy for details.

OPEN SOURCE
Marmalade TTS is open source. The source code is MIT-licensed; the published store build links espeak-ng and is therefore distributed as a GPL-3.0 combined work, with full corresponding source available in the project repository.
