Marmalade TTS 1.3.4, a bug-fix release.
- Setup is readable with Android 16's dark theme, and Back steps back a step.
- Voices read through abbreviations and initials like "Dr. Smith" instead of stopping.
- Better reading of times, prices, "#1", "C#" and "<3".
- Routing an app to another alias works again after you delete the alias it used.
- Apps that look for the default voice always find one.
- Stopping a cloud voice is instant.
- Small fixes.
