Marmalade TTS 1.0.0, the first stable release.
- Speaks offline from first launch with the built-in Kitten Nano voice.
- Kokoro speaks 8 languages with automatic detection; Pocket TTS adds expressive English voices; optional cloud voices with your own keys.
- Full system TTS engine with TalkBack support and per-app voices.
- 22 effect presets plus an editor, voice aliases, emoji-driven expression.
- Private by default: no ads, no accounts, no analytics.
