Norwegian (bokmål) voice review — marmalade-tts

We're adding Norwegian voices from the National Library of Norway's open Voice Control Corpus (CC0). Honest caveat up front: this corpus was recorded in ordinary meeting rooms, not a studio, so these voices are rougher than our other languages — they will be labeled accordingly in the app. We'd like to know whether they're useful despite that ("something is better than nothing"), or not. None of the maintainers speak Norwegian, so before these ship we'd like native speakers to listen and tell us what's good enough. All audio below is synthesized on-device-equivalent output from marmalade-tts's VITS engine.

How to give feedback: comment on the Norwegian (bokmål) issue. Most useful: which sample ids sound wrong and what is wrong (mispronounced word, weird rhythm, wrong stress), an overall 1–5 naturalness rating per voice — and better test sentences than these (this is a small starter set written by non-speakers; corrections welcome).

Questions for reviewers

  1. Naturalness 1–5 per voice? Any mispronounced words (sample # + word)?

  3. Are the test sentences themselves wrong anywhere? Suggest better ones.
