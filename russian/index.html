Russian (accented) voice review — marmalade-tts

There is no native Russian voice yet that meets our licensing and provenance bar. In the meantime, our Ukrainian voices can speak Russian with a Ukrainian accent (the text is transliterated into the Ukrainian alphabet before synthesis). In an automated intelligibility check this scored in the same range as our German accented fallback, which German reviewers rated tolerable. But an automated check can't judge the accent — Russian speakers, please tell us whether this is usable.

How to give feedback: comment on issue #1. Most useful: is the accent charming, tolerable, or unusable? Which sample ids sound wrong and what is wrong (mispronounced word, weird rhythm, wrong stress), an overall 1–5 naturalness rating per voice — and better test sentences than these (this is a small starter set written by non-speakers; corrections welcome).

Questions for reviewers

  1. Is the Ukrainian accent charming, tolerable, or unusable for daily reading?
  2. Naturalness 1–5 per voice? Any mispronounced words (sample # + word)?
  3. Are the test sentences themselves wrong anywhere? Suggest better ones.
  4. Anything Russian-specific we should test (numbers, dates, names)?
